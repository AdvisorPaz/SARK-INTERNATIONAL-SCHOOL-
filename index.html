About SARK International School

Established in 2012, SARK International School is located in Madhepura, Bihar, and offers classes from Nursery to Class 10.

With English as the medium of instruction, the school aims to provide a supportive environment for learning, creativity and personal development.

Our school information and facilities will be reviewed with the school management before publication.
